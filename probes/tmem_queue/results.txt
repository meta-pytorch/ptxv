Reading: measured nulls without a discriminating control. A thread's STTM after its own
un-waited LDTM of the same words never changed what the LDTM returned (mode 0); of two
un-fenced STTMs of one thread to the same words the second always won (mode 4); an LDTM after
the thread's own un-fenced STTM read the new value (mode 2); 1.2e10 words each. Mode 3 (warp 4
announces its load, sleeps 2 us and loads warp 0's words; warp 0 stores to them on the
announcement) shows only that the check reads the other value when the store lands first in
real time: the store precedes the load by about 2 us, so it is not a reordering control, and
nothing here shows the setup could see a store overtaking an earlier load of the same queue,
two stores swapping, or a load overtaking a store. The model assumes none of the three orders:
the first two are `tmem-queue` contract findings at the strict level and under the GB300
profile, the third a race.
