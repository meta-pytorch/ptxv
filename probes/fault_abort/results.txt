Result: `launch result: 700 (an illegal memory access was encountered) after 106.3 ms`.
The grid was aborted at the fault: the warp-mates neither waited forever at the BSYNC nor
spun forever on the flag. A fault therefore never leaves other threads waiting for the
faulting one; ptxv's derived deadlock of lanes parked on a faulted lane (forum post 76, MISS
175) is not a GB300 behaviour, and the explorer now releases them (exec.rs
`release_after_stop`). The over-read of MISS 175 (an emulated 16-bit atomic's 4-byte access
reaching 2 bytes past an exact-size allocation, inside the allocation granule) does not fault
on GB300 at all; ptxv still reports it as `fault` (outside the allocation the case declares).
